How It Works

Every conflict is different. The process is simple.

How the work begins.

Every situation is different.

Some clients need a single strategic conversation. Others need ongoing perspective over time.

The work begins with a short initial conversation. If it feels like the right fit, we decide together what level of involvement makes sense.

Some situations benefit from perspective immediately. Others require deeper ongoing work.

Part of the work is slowing situations down enough for perspective to return.

The Process
01
Initial conversation

A brief discussion to understand:

  • the situation
  • the dynamics
  • what feels unresolved
  • whether Resolve is the right fit
02
Perspective and strategy

Clarifying:

  • communication
  • timing
  • emotional dynamics
  • risks
  • next steps

Helping clients think more clearly before acting reactively.

03
Ongoing advisory, when needed

Some situations resolve quickly. Others unfold over time.

Support may remain:

  • occasional
  • strategic
  • active
  • behind the scenes

depending on the situation.

Selectivity

Resolve is intentionally selective about the situations and people it works with.

Not every conflict is appropriate for this kind of advisory relationship.

The work depends on:

  • honesty
  • openness
  • discipline
  • willingness to pursue clarity rather than escalation
Engagement

All engagements begin with a short initial conversation.

If there appears to be a strong fit, work typically begins with a minimum engagement of $2,600, which may be applied toward ongoing advisory work.
