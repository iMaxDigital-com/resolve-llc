How It Works


Every conflict is different. The process is simple.

How the work begins.

Every situation is different.

Some clients need a single strategic conversation. Others benefit from ongoing guidance over time.

The work begins with a short initial conversation.

Most people contact Resolve when a conflict has become the central problem. The purpose of the conversation is to understand the situation, answer questions, and determine whether I can help.

I work with a limited number of clients and do not accept every engagement.

If there is a fit, we discuss the most appropriate next steps.

The goal is not immediate answers.

The goal is clarity before escalation.

Ongoing Advisory

Some situations resolve quickly.

Others unfold over time. Support may continue behind the scenes as circumstances evolve.

Selectivity

Resolve is intentionally selective.

Not every situation is appropriate for this kind of advisory relationship, and not every engagement is accepted.

The work is most effective when clients approach a situation with:

  • honesty
  • openness
  • discipline
  • a genuine desire for clarity rather than escalation
Engagement

All engagements begin with a Short Initial Conversation at no charge.

There is no typical engagement. Some clients need a single strategic conversation. Others benefit from ongoing advisory support over weeks or months as a situation develops.

Most client engagements involve several conversations over a period of time and typically range between $2,500 and $5,000.

Some matters involving businesses, ownership interests, family wealth, investor relationships, or other significant financial considerations are broader in scope and may require a more extensive engagement.

For situations that do not require a broader engagement, Office Hours are available at $650 per hour.
