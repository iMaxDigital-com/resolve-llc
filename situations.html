Situations

Every situation is unique.

While every situation is unique, these are some of the circumstances that most often lead people to seek help.

Business Disputes
Disagreements between partners involving trust, communication, decision-making, ownership, or the future direction of a business.
Leadership and Ownership Changes
Founder conflict, succession challenges, leadership transitions, governance questions, or competing visions for the future.
Family Conflict
Estrangement, inheritance disputes, communication breakdowns, caregiving responsibilities, and long-standing family tensions.
Divorce and Separation
Complex financial, parenting, and relationship dynamics that benefit from calm judgment and thoughtful strategy.
Investor Conflicts
Tensions involving investors, capital partners, financial expectations, control, transparency, or alignment of interests.
Ongoing Advisory
Situations that benefit from ongoing perspective and strategic guidance over time.
